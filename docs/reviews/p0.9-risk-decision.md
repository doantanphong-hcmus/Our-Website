| Residual level | Count |

M0 proves that the chosen stack can provide basic Places data, free AI,
ordered realtime state, and a public Git-driven deployment without a personal
server or automatic paid fallback. Development may continue into P1.

repository is currently public, spike credentials have been shared through
development channels, and account recovery controls remain unverified. Those
items form a hard security gate at the start of P1.

## Decision and conditions

### GO now

- Start P1 design foundation, app shell, schema design and test harness work.
- Keep nullable Places fields, no price level, no billing provider and manual
  fallback.
- Keep Workers AI with local validation, three-deck/day cap and reviewed
  fallback.
- Keep Durable Objects for ordering/realtime and D1 as business source of
  truth.

### NO-GO until cleared

Do not add real accounts, personal fixtures, production secrets or private
media until all four conditions pass:

1. Repository visibility is private.
2. All spike credentials are revoked/rotated and absent from Git history.
3. GitHub and Cloudflare ownership, MFA and recovery are owner-verified.
4. Password hashing fits Workers Free CPU limits without weakening security.

Places-dependent P2 work additionally requires the owner-controlled Geoapify
key, real usage area coverage, current attribution and Free-plan terms.

## Timeline adjustment

- Keep the **20 implementation weeks** as the working baseline.
- Add a **two-week management reserve**, making the realistic forecast
  **20–22 weeks**, followed by the already planned seven-day stabilization.
- Reserve the first day of week 2 for repository privacy, credential rotation
  and account ownership; unresolved items slip the schedule day-for-day.
- Validate auth CPU before P1.5, deployed realtime before closing P1.10, and DO
  preview topology before closing P1.15.
- Complete real-area Places validation before P2.4; it does not block P1.

## FLAGS

> Immediate owner actions: make the repository private, rotate spike
> credentials, and complete the non-secret MFA/recovery checklist.

> Engineering must not solve a Free-tier limit by silently enabling billing or
> weakening authentication, privacy, validation, accessibility or acceptance.
